**Collagen peptides** are short amino-acid chains formed when collagen is hydrolyzed. After oral intake they are digested further, absorbed into the circulation, and can reach the skin.[^1]

## Healing Properties

### Skin Health

A 2026 narrative review of human studies of oral collagen or collagen peptides reported improvements in several skin biophysical measures, including stratum corneum hydration, epidermal permeability barrier function, elasticity, firmness, and roughness.[^1]

Proposed mechanisms include upregulation of keratinocyte differentiation, epidermal lipid synthesis, hyaluronic acid and collagen synthesis, inhibition of inflammation, stimulation of fibroblast proliferation, and increased aquaporin 3 expression.[^1]

- The included human studies were heterogeneous and often small, so the review does not support firm conclusions that oral collagen peptides improve cutaneous biophysical properties.[^1]
- The review could not rank collagen sources or peptide types by efficacy; further work is needed on source, type, and individual skin outcomes.[^1]
